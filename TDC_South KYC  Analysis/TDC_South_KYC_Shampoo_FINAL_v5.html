Discovery vs choice — how to read it
Discovery: Influencer/Creator (12), Instagram (10), Advertisement (9), Google/Search (8).

Choice: Reviews/Ratings (6), Recommendation (4), Product claims (4). The two questions answer different jobs; they should not be merged into one “channel” ranking.

3 most important insights

Built from cross-question relationships first, then checked against state and profile patterns. These are the findings most defensible from this sample.

01

The real retention switch is not “working” — it is getting the scalp to a resolved state.

32 / 38felt some effect
16 / 38completely resolved
16 / 16resolved → positive repurchase
0 / 8still dandruff → positive repurchase
What it means: Consumers can say the shampoo is working while still carrying the problem. Commercially, “dandruff is gone / under control now” is a much stronger retention signal than a vague sense that the formula is working.
High confidence within sample
02

Earlier visible improvement is associated with a better current scalp outcome.

19 / 221–2 week group improved
7 / 122–4 week group improved
86%improved in 1–2 weeks
58%improved in 2–4 weeks
What it means: Speed of first noticeable change appears to matter. This is a correlation, not proof that faster response causes better outcomes; severity, usage pattern and other factors are not controlled.
Medium confidence · directional
03

The consumer is buying a concrete problem solution, and the retention reasons match the original problem.

29flake / visibility mentions
21repurchase mentions: controls itching
20repurchase mentions: controls dandruff
74%positive repurchase intent
What it means: The strongest product story is functional: visible dandruff and scalp discomfort bring the consumer in; dandruff and itch control are what justify buying again. Texture and brand-entry channels matter, but they are secondary to the core problem being solved.
High confidence within sample

What the analysis says to trust — and what not to overclaim

Trust more

  • Q3 × Q6: working perception tracks current scalp outcome very closely.
  • Q6 × Q7: current scalp status has the clearest relationship with repurchase.
  • Q1 problem signals and Q7 reason coding point to the same functional job: dandruff / itch control.
  • State patterns in Telangana and Tamil Nadu are more informative than the tiny Kerala/Karnataka/Andhra bases.

Do not overread

  • Q5 durability: only 11 explicit entries.
  • Discovery-channel repurchase rates: overlapping channels and tiny cells make them unstable.
  • State “100%” results in Kerala, Karnataka or Andhra Pradesh: bases are only 3, 3 and 1.
  • Any causal claim that faster results, a channel or a tier caused satisfaction or repurchase.
